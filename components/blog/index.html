F08 · Research

Myth vs Fact

Evidence-led correction panels

Claim: "There is only one universal way to perform every ritual."

Different communities, temples and families may preserve different practices. Explain the context and cite evidence before offering any correction.

How should evidence be presented?

Show source strength, regional scope, and the reason for the correction. Never ridicule a devotional practice.
