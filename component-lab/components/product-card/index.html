SP-PROD production contract: one canonical Shopify snippet should render product title, canonical URL, featured media, price, compare-at price and availability from the product object. “New” is an explicit merchandising flag; sale and sold-out states come from product data. Wishlist appears only when the workflow is wired. Secondary media is optional and never carries critical information.
Media ruleCanonical 1:1 frame, consistent object fit, lazy-loaded below the fold. Optional second image only on hover-capable devices.
Price ruleCurrent price leads. Compare-at price is secondary and only appears when Shopify reports a genuine reduction.
Title ruleDo not shrink or silently truncate meaningful product names. Cards expand naturally for longer titles.
State ruleAvailability must be explicit. Sold-out products remain reachable; the card must never imply they can be purchased.
Touch ruleWishlist and primary actions target roughly 44px on narrow layouts with visible keyboard focus.
Reuse ruleThe same card primitive powers homepage grids, collections, recommendations and full search results.
